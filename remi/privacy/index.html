We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect someone's safety.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • Export your conversations in the app (Settings → Export).
  • Delete your account and all its data in the app (Settings → Account → Delete).
  • Turn off notifications per friend or entirely, in the app or in your device settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and withdraw consent at any time.

Email helloworld@gonglab.app for anything the app cannot do. We reply within one month. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Security

We encrypt data in transit, limit who can access production systems, and keep access logs. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will tell you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. About your AI friends

Your friends in Remi are AI characters, not real people. Their replies are generated automatically and can be wrong. Remi is not a source of medical, legal or financial advice, and it is not an emergency service. If you are in danger, contact local emergency services.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows the latest version.
