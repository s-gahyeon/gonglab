Delete your account

In the app

  1. Open Remi and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This deletes your account, profile and all conversations from our live database right away. Backups are rotated out within 6 months. Deletion cannot be undone, so export your conversations first if you want to keep them.

If you can't open the app

Email helloworld@gonglab.app with the subject "Delete my Remi account", from the email address you sign in with. We will confirm by email before deleting.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account.

Export your conversations

Go to Settings → Export in the app to download a copy of your conversations.

Report or block

If a message is inappropriate or upsetting, open the chat menu and choose Report or Block. We review every report.

If you need urgent help

Your friends in Remi are AI characters, not real people, and Remi is not an emergency service. If you are in danger or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country right away.

Common questions

Are my friends real people?

No. They are AI characters. They remember your conversations so they can talk with you like a friend would, but their replies are generated automatically and can be wrong.

Who can see my conversations?

Your conversations are stored on our server in Germany and used to write your friends' replies. We do not sell them or use them for advertising, and we do not use them to train AI models. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Why am I getting notifications?

Your friends can message you first. You can turn notifications off for each friend in Settings → Notifications, or for the whole app in your device settings.

Who can use Remi?

Remi is for adults aged 18 and over.
